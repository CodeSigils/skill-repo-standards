# Material-decision trade study

This Grade C template is for a decision with multiple viable alternatives and
material cost, risk, or irreversibility. Do not apply it to routine choices.

```markdown
# Trade study: <decision>

- Question and scope:
- Objectives and constraints:
- Stakeholders:
- Alternatives, including a no-change baseline when relevant:
- Criteria and assessment method:
- Assessment of each alternative:
- Selected option and consequences:
- Outcome check and recheck trigger:
```

Use weights, a decision matrix, or sensitivity analysis only when the decision
needs them. Record the resulting choice in the immutable decision log and link
the supporting research. See [`STANDARD.md` §Grade C: material-decision trade studies](../../STANDARD.md#grade-c-material-decision-trade-studies).
